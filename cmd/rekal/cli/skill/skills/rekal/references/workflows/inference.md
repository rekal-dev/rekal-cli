# Supported-inference workflow

Use this lens only when the answer requested is explicitly qualified: likely,
possible, expected, suggested, or inferable. It does not relax the evidence
standard for ordinary factual questions.

**Evidence contract**

- The actor and proposition being inferred stay fixed; a nearby capability,
  preference, event, or another person's circumstances is not a substitute.
- Direct premises come from drilled evidence, not from searching only for the
  expected conclusion.
- Observed facts remain distinct from plans, hypotheticals, jokes, negations,
  and unsupported speculation.
- The conclusion is no stronger than its premises: likely is not certain, and
  possible is not confirmed. Counterevidence and later state changes matter.

Useful operations include searching on the entity plus supporting fact and
checking later turns for correction or counterevidence.

If the premises do not support the inference, preserve the gap rather than
turning topical association into evidence. Answer the qualified conclusion only.
